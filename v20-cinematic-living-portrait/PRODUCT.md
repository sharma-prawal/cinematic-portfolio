# V20 Cinematic Living Portrait

A local-only React/TypeScript/Vite experiment to evaluate the supplied Google Flow Variation 2 as a photographic portfolio hero. Native 24 fps video is the authoritative image. No face reconstruction, cursor seeking, segmentation, image generation, or promotion into the parent portfolio.

Experience mode: the portrait leads; controls recede. Desktop is primary; touch disables pointer parallax. Reduced motion uses the first photographic frame and stops playback. All source files outside this directory remain immutable.

Three variants: A native video baseline; B bounded whole-frame translation; C visible studio atmosphere outside the opaque video and a slight caption translation. Debug controls exist only in development with ?debug=1.
